Placeholder guide

Use these tags anywhere in a subject or body. They fill in automatically when you generate an email. In the editor, click a chip to insert one.

PlaceholderFills with

HYPERLINKS
To turn words into a clickable link, wrap the visible text in square brackets and put the URL in parentheses right after:
book a 15-minute Parent Conference using [this link](https://calendly.com/scholars/parent-conference) to schedule a time
The words this link become the clickable link. When you copy the body or export to Word, it stays a real hyperlink (the URL stays hidden behind the text). Use full URLs starting with https:// or mailto:.
PRONOUNS — OPTIONAL (driven by the Gender field)
